# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners and small property owners in and around Lorain County who want a new lawn or a yard overhaul, and need a contractor they can trust.

They are usually deciding quickly, often on mobile, comparing options, and looking for confidence signals: workmanship, responsiveness, clear scope, and straightforward next steps.

## Product Purpose

Turn site visits into qualified leads for YardBros Landscaping by making it effortless to:

- understand what YardBros does (services, outcomes, process)
- request a quote with minimal friction
- get fast help via chat when questions block a decision

Success looks like: more quote requests, fewer abandoned forms, and higher-intent conversations through chat.

## Brand Personality

Premium, modern, design-forward.

## Anti-references

- Generic contractor templates (stock grass hero, loud green gradients, “call now” clutter)
- Low-trust patterns: hidden contact info, confusing navigation, aggressive popups

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over hype: show capability with clarity, not noise.
2. Reduce hesitation: make the next step obvious and low-effort.
3. Craft is the brand: details, alignment, typography, and motion communicate quality.
4. Calm confidence: premium presence without “luxury theater”.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG AA.
- Support reduced motion and maintain visible focus states across marketing, forms, and chat.
